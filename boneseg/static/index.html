Tips

  • Click adds a point of the current kind. Shift-click adds the other kind. Right-click removes the nearest point.
  • 1 and 2 switch between object and background. Ctrl+Z undoes the last point.
  • , and . move through slices. F fits the image. Scroll to zoom and drag with the middle mouse button or Space held down to pan.
  • M, H and U toggle the mask, heatmap and uncertainty overlays.
  • The orange overlay shows where the mask is unstable. The dashed ring suggests where one more click helps most.
  • Save good clicks as a profile to segment new images of the same stain without clicking.
