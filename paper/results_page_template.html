Fine-tuning DINOv2

The last 4 of DINOv2 Small's 12 blocks were trained on expert slices, with early stopping on separate validation slices. The baseline is the same code with DINOv2 left frozen. Click results use a backbone fine-tuned on the other three samples.

Frozen DINOv2Fine-tuned DINOv2

Fine-tuning makes 3 + 6 clicks almost as good as 25 + 25 clicks, on all four samples. It changes nothing once there are enough clicks or labels. Trained on the sample's own slices, it helped on A, E and F and hurt on C.

Osteoclasts on public data

The NOISe data has brightfield images of TRAP-stained mouse osteoclast cultures in five batches, with every osteoclast outlined by experts. An osteoclast is a purple cell with three or more nuclei. Each batch was held out in turn, with every setting tuned on the other four. A cell counts as found when a predicted cell overlaps the expert outline by at least half (IoU 0.5).

Clicks

There are about four osteoclasts per patch. Before the run, the targets for counting were set at ICC ≥ 0.8 and bias within 10%. Tuned for finding cells, boneseg over-counts by 0.8 per patch. With the app's default settings, fixed on the bone data beforehand, 10 + 20 clicks count within +7% at ICC 0.81, just inside both targets.

What boneseg's extra and missing cells are

On development patches, splitting and merging of osteoclasts is rare. Most errors are look-alikes: clusters of small stained cells and pale large cells that the experts do not count, and 84 of 400 faint or thin osteoclasts that are missed. Telling them apart needs the nucleus count, which clicks alone cannot convey.

U-Nets in the research notebook

The notebook's Kaggle run trained U-Nets on five samples, with labels from other samples, and compared them with DINOv2 Small clicks.

A U-Net on the image alone learns little from so few labels. Given DINOv2's score map, it only has to sharpen the edges and does well. The app's learned refiner is a light version of the image-plus-score-map U-Net. It helps with few or careless clicks and costs a little with many, so it is off by default.

The patch classifier involves no U-Net. DINOv2 Small stays frozen and describes every 14-pixel patch with 384 numbers. A small classifier learns from labelled slices which descriptions mean bone. It is either one linear layer, like logistic regression, or a two-layer network with 64 hidden units, optionally also given the average description of the 5 × 5 neighbouring patches. Cross-validation picks the variant. Its patch-level output is upsampled to full resolution and thresholded at 0.5. The app's learned model is the same idea.

This run evaluated DINOv2 Small with settings tuned for DINOv2 Giant, which understates Small. A corrected run with per-backbone tuning is ready to push to Kaggle.
